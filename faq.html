Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about BizLedger. Cannot find yours? Contact us.

What is BizLedger?

BizLedger is a digital khata (udhar ledger) app for small shops. You record udhar and jama, send reminders, make bills, manage stock and see reports.

Is the app available now?

The Android app is coming soon. Please check this page or contact us for updates.

How much does it cost?

Basic is ₹299 per month and Premium is ₹499 per month. If you pay for a full year you get one month free (₹3,289 and ₹5,489). Taxes as applicable.

How do I pay?

Plan payments are made securely through Razorpay using UPI, debit/credit card or netbanking. We never see or store your card or bank details.

Do plans renew automatically?

No. A plan covers a fixed monthly or yearly period and does not renew automatically. You choose when to pay again.

Does BizLedger hold my customers' payments?

No. When a customer pays you by UPI, the money goes directly to your bank account. BizLedger does not hold or move that money.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in some cases, for example a double payment or a payment where the plan was not activated. See our Refund & Cancellation policy.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored in Google Cloud data centres in India (Mumbai). You sign in with Google and can protect the app with a PIN. Read our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Profile and choose delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.

How can I contact support?
