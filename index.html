Khata for your customers

Customers scan your QR, enter their PIN and see their own ledger. They can pay by UPI and tell you, you approve the request.

Fast billing that fits a counter

Search an item or scan its barcode, take cash with change calculator, or put the rest on udhar. Hold a bill and come back to it, take returns with a credit note, send quotations, and close the day. GST billing and GST report are on Premium.

Offers for your customers

Staff (munim) access

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Full answers are on the FAQ page.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Google sign-in, an optional app-lock PIN or fingerprint, and your data is stored in India. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I pay for a plan?

Only through Google Play, inside the app. There is no other payment link. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the Play Store.

Can my customers see their khata?

Yes. They scan your QR, enter the PIN you give them and see their own ledger in read-only mode. They can also see your offers.

How do I delete my account?

Any time. The steps are on the Delete account page.
