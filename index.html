BizLedger

A digital khata (udhar ledger) app for small shops in India. Shop owners record udhar and jama, send reminders, and share the khata with each customer through a QR code.

About us

BizLedger is built and operated by MotionCircuit, based in Panipat, Haryana, India. We make simple tools that replace the paper khata for dukandars, so that credit and payments are recorded clearly and customers can see their own balance.

What the app does

  • Shop owners sign in with Google, add customers, and record udhar and jama entries.
  • Customers scan the shop's QR code to see their own khata (read-only).
  • Reminders and notifications for due dates, plus bills, products, expenses and reports.
  • UPI payment details on reminders. Money goes directly from the customer to the shop owner. BizLedger does not hold it.

Plans and pricing

Shop owners subscribe to a paid plan (monthly or yearly) to use the app. Prices are shown inside the app before you pay. Online payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card or bank details.
