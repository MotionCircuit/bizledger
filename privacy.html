Privacy Policy

Last updated: 06 November 2026

This policy explains what BizLedger (the "app") collects, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who we are

BizLedger is operated by MotionCircuit (Proprietor: Nitin), Noorwala, Panipat, Haryana 132103, India. Contact: contact.whonithin@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account: name, email and profile photo from your Google sign-in.
  • Shop owners: shop name, phone, address, UPI ID, optional shop logo, and the ledger you record (customers, udhar and jama entries, bills, products, expenses).
  • Customers (people whose khata is kept): name and phone as entered by the shop owner, and the entries of that khata.
  • Payments: monthly plan payments are processed by Razorpay. We store the payment ID, amount and plan. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  • UPI payment requests: the reference number a customer types when they say they have paid. The shop owner approves or rejects it.
  • Device: a push-notification token (per device), app crash reports and basic usage analytics (Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics).
  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes. Photos are not uploaded.
  • Advertising (customer side only): Google AdMob may show a banner. Where required (EU/UK), you are asked for consent first and can change it later in the app.

3. How we use it

To run the ledger, send you reminders and notifications you asked for, activate your plan, prevent misuse (for example, guessing a customer PIN), fix crashes, and show ads on the customer side. We do not sell your data.

4. Who we share it with

Firebase / Google Cloud (hosting, database, authentication, notifications, crash reports), Razorpay (payments), Google AdMob (customer-side ads). Shop owners can see their own customers and entries; customers see only the khata linked to their own login.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in Google Cloud data centres in India (Mumbai, India (asia-south1)). The app lock PIN is stored only on your phone, as a salted hash. Customer link PINs are checked on the server and locked after repeated wrong attempts.

6. How long we keep it

While your account is active. After you request deletion, your data is removed after a 7-day grace period (see section 8). Backups are deleted within 30 days.

7. Your choices

You can edit your data in the app, turn off notifications in Profile, use the app lock, and withdraw ad consent in the app where offered.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → "Account delete karo". Or open the deletion instructions. The request is processed after 7 days. You can cancel during those 7 days.

9. Children

BizLedger is for business owners and their customers and is not directed at children under 18.

10. Changes

We will show a notice in the app when this policy changes in a material way.
