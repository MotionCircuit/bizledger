Delete your BizLedger account

BizLedger is operated by MotionCircuit, Panipat, Haryana, India.

Deletion removes your data permanently after a 7-day grace period. During those 7 days you can cancel.

Option 1: from the app (recommended)

  1. Open BizLedger and sign in with the same Google account.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Account delete karo.
  3. Confirm. You will be signed out.
  4. To cancel, sign in again and open Profile → Delete request cancel karo within 7 days.

Option 2: without the app

Email contact.whonithin@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Google account, with the subject "Account deletion request". We will verify the request and process it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Shop owners: the shop, customers, entries, bills, products, expenses, staff list and login.
  • Customers: your links to shops and your login. The shop keeps its own record of the khata.

Payment records needed for accounting or legal reasons may be kept for the period the law requires.
