About BizLedger

BizLedger is a digital khata app made for the small shops that run on udhar — kirana stores, general stores and local businesses across India.

Why we built it

Paper khata gets lost, pages get torn, and it is hard to remember who owes what. BizLedger keeps every udhar and jama entry in one place, so the shop owner always knows the balance and can remind customers politely and on time.

What it does

  • Records udhar and jama for every customer.
  • Sends payment reminders with the pending amount and your UPI QR.
  • Lets customers view their own khata in read-only mode.
  • Makes bills, manages products and stock, and tracks shop expenses.
  • Shows simple reports of your business.

Who runs it

BizLedger is operated by MotionCircuit (Proprietor: Nitin), Noorwala, Panipat, Haryana 132103, India

Your money stays yours

When a customer pays you by UPI, the money goes directly from the customer to your bank account. BizLedger never holds or moves that money. We only charge a subscription for using the app, paid through Razorpay.
