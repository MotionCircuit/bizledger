Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 08 November 2026

1. Cancellation

Plans cover a fixed monthly or yearly period and do not renew automatically. You can stop using a plan at any time; access lasts until the paid period ends.

2. When you can get a refund

  • Payment was taken but the plan was not activated.
  • You were charged twice for the same period.
  • You request a refund within 7 days of your first payment and have not used the paid features.

Each request is reviewed.

3. When a refund is not given

  • Plans that have been used for most of the paid period.
  • Accounts suspended for breaking the Terms and Conditions.

4. How to request a refund

Email contact.whonithin@gmail.com with your sign-in email, payment ID and the reason. We reply within 2 working days.

5. Refund timing

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method (UPI, card or netbanking) within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take longer to show it.

6. Delivery

BizLedger is a digital service only. Plans are activated in your account right after the payment succeeds. Nothing is shipped.
