Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 08 November 2026. This policy applies to BizLedger plans bought through Razorpay.

1. Cancellation

Plans are paid for a fixed period (monthly or yearly) and do not renew automatically. You are never charged again unless you choose to pay again. You can stop using the plan at any time, and access continues until the paid period ends.

2. When you get a refund

  • Money was deducted but the plan was not activated, and we cannot activate it.
  • You were charged twice for the same period (duplicate payment).
  • You request a refund within 7 days of your first payment and have not used the paid features. We will review each request.

3. When we do not refund

Plans that have been used for most of the paid period, and accounts suspended for breaking the Terms and Conditions, are not refunded.

4. How to request a refund

Email contact.whonithin@gmail.com with your sign-in email, the payment ID and the reason. We reply within 2 working days.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or net banking) within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

6. Delivery

BizLedger is a digital service. There is no physical shipping. The plan is activated in your account right after the payment succeeds.
