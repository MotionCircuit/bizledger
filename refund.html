Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 08 November 2026

1. Cancellation

Plans are bought through Google Play as monthly or yearly subscriptions. You can cancel at any time in Play Store → Subscriptions; access lasts until the paid period ends.

2. When you can get a refund

  • Payment was taken but the plan was not activated.
  • You were charged twice for the same period.
  • You request a refund within 7 days of your first payment and have not used the paid features.

Each request is reviewed.

3. When a refund is not given

  • Plans that have been used for most of the paid period.
  • Accounts suspended for breaking the Terms and Conditions.

4. How to request a refund

Payments are taken by Google Play, so refunds for a Play purchase follow Google Play's refund process. For a plan that was not activated or a double charge, email contact.whonithin@gmail.com with your sign-in email, Google Play order ID and the reason. We reply within 2 working days.

5. Refund timing

Approved refunds are returned by Google Play to the original payment method within the time Google states. Your bank may take longer to show it.

6. Delivery

BizLedger is a digital service only. Plans are activated in your account right after Google Play confirms the purchase. Nothing is shipped.
